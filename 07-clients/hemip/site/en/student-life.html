Student life

Thesis defenses, company visits, field trips, celebrations: at HEMIP, learning also happens outside the classroom.

Thesis defenses

Each year, our final-year students present their dissertations and capstone projects to a panel of lecturers and professionals: a highlight that crowns three years of work.

Company visits

To connect theory and practice, the Academic Affairs Department organises guided visits to our partners: refinery, plants, banks, power generation sites.

Field trips

Educational outings and field discoveries: industrial sites, dams, Congo Basin rivers… to see for real what we study in class.

School celebrations

Welcome day, end-of-year parties, graduation ceremonies: life at HEMIP is also about moments shared by students, lecturers and families.

Internships and careers

From internship to job

HEMIP partners with many companies in Pointe-Noire and Brazzaville where our students go on guided visits and internships. Depending on the skills shown, an internship can lead to a job: several of our interns have already signed fixed-term and permanent contracts.

Services for companies

Companies, public bodies and project owners: HEMIP puts its research unit’s expertise at your service.

Our areas of work

Expertise for companies

Energy and hydrocarbons

  • Semi-industrial production: catalysts, petrol, kerosene, LPG, biogas
  • Biofuels: biodiesel, bioethanol
  • Thermal, solar and hydro power

Potential clients : CORAF, Eni, AOGC, Perenco, Trident, SNPC, TotalEnergies, E2C

Mining and mineral processing

  • Mining business plans
  • Optimal extraction projects: gold, coltan, copper, cobalt, palladium, platinum
  • Limestone processing for cement and chalk

Potential clients : Anglogold, GCM, CILU, Dangote

Industrial engineering

  • Semi-industrial production: drinking water, chalk, paints, cement, detergents, bio-fertilisers
  • Software development
  • Water drilling and solar PV installation

Potential clients : Saris Congo, Dangote, La Congolaise des Eaux, farmers, builders

Consulting and training

  • Short courses and capacity building
  • Technical studies and engineering projects for companies
  • Environmental studies (ESIA, ESMP)

Potential clients : Oil, chemical and food companies, universities

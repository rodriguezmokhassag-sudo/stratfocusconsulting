Our mission

Teach, research, employ

Train professionals

Provide high-quality training in engineering (senior technicians) and management (Bachelor’s graduates), with qualified, professional and innovative lecturers.

Useful research

Conduct applied scientific research, within a dedicated unit, to deliver solutions suited to society’s problems.

Prepare for work

Prepare students for careers in the primary (Perenco, TotalEnergies…), secondary (CORAF, Dangote, Saris…) and tertiary sectors (E2C, La Congolaise des Eaux…), or to launch their own start-up.

The campus

In downtown Pointe-Noire

The campus is located downtown, behind the Mayombe Tower, and easy to reach by public transport. It houses classrooms, chemistry, process engineering and computer labs, as well as electromechanics and electronics workshops.

  • Teaching under the LMD system
  • Day and evening sessions
  • Member of the African Order of Grandes Écoles and Universities (ORAGEU)

Research and innovation

A process engineering research unit

Beyond teaching, HEMIP is a hub of innovation. Our research unit designs practical solutions to society’s problems: renewable energy, biofuels, plastic recycling, water treatment.

  • Renewable energy — solar PV, biomass, micro hydropower
  • Biofuels — biodiesel and bioethanol
  • Circular economy — turning plastic waste into value
  • Drinking water — industrial surface water treatment

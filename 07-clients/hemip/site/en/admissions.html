Admissions

Join HEMIP La Percée: pre-register online and our admissions office will guide you at every step.

How to apply

Four simple steps

Online pre-registration

Fill in the form below: your request reaches our WhatsApp directly.

Talk to admissions

We call you back to advise you and list the documents needed.

Submit your file

You bring your complete file to the campus, behind the Mayombe Tower.

Final enrolment

Once approved, choose your session (day or evening) and start classes.

Who can apply?

Holders of the baccalauréat or an equivalent diploma, for entry into the Bachelor’s programs. Working students can attend evening classes.

Scholarships

Scholarships are available for the most deserving students. Ask about them when you pre-register.

Tuition fees

Registration and tuition fees are provided by our admissions office on request.
